Trading Risk Management

Trading Risk Management

Risk management is the part of trading you fully control. BattlexJournal tracks the core numbers — risk per trade, daily risk, drawdown, trade frequency — automatically from your journal, and checks them before you enter each trade.

The numbers that define your risk

Every risk system reduces to a handful of limits. BattlexJournal tracks them per account, from the trades in your journal:

  • Risk per trade — the dollar amount at risk on each position, derived from entry, stop and size.
  • Daily risk budget — the total risk you planned for the day, with used and remaining amounts tracked live.
  • Daily loss limit — the maximum loss you allow per day; when it is hit, the engine stops you from trading further that day.
  • Drawdown — current and maximum drawdown from your equity curve, compared against your drawdown limit.
  • Trade count — your planned maximum trades per day versus the actual count.
  • Max open risk — the total risk across open positions, so a new trade cannot stack unlimited concurrent exposure.

Status bands and the pre-trade check

From those limits, BattlexJournal derives a risk status for your account — normal, caution, high or limit — and runs a pre-trade check every time you plan an entry:

  • Clear — the trade fits within your limits.
  • Caution — you are approaching a limit; the check tells you which one.
  • Violation — the trade would break one of your rules; it is flagged on entry.
  • Blocked — the trade would exceed a hard limit (daily loss limit, drawdown limit, max open risk); the entry is rejected with the reason.

This is where risk management stops being a spreadsheet you update on Sundays and becomes a check that runs on every trade, before the decision is final.

Risk events and notifications

Risk breaches and near-breaches are recorded as events — limit breached, high-risk state, loss limit reached — and surface in your risk feed and notifications. You do not need to notice the limit yourself; the engine flags the moment your trading pushes against it.

Trade frequency as a risk

The most underestimated risk input is frequency. More trades means more opportunity to give back profits, and overtrading often follows a loss. BattlexJournal tracks your daily trade count against your plan and includes frequency as a scored dimension in trading discipline.

The review loop

Limits are only useful if you review whether they fit. Your risk history — how often you approached or hit each limit — is part of the daily, weekly and monthly review cycle derived from your journal. If you are always at the edge of your daily loss limit, the review makes that visible, and you can adjust the limit or the behavior. See how to review your trades.

Where it all comes from

Every risk number is computed from your trading journal — the same ledger your analytics read. Log the trade, and risk, discipline and performance all update together. The performance analytics page shows how your returns compare to the risk you took.
