Day Trading Journal

Trading Journal for Day Traders

Day trading generates dozens of decisions in a single session — and almost no memory of them. A day trading journal logs each trade, session and setup, tracks your risk and behavior, and turns the day's chaos into data you can review tonight.

Why day traders need a journal more than anyone

Day traders make more decisions per day than any other trader, which means more chances to repeat the same mistake. Three problems are unique to day trading:

  • Volume hides patterns. Twenty trades a day feels like evidence, but without a record the only thing you remember is the last trade.
  • Sessions are short. The open, the first hour, the midday lull and the close behave completely differently. You cannot learn the differences without recording the session on every trade.
  • Revenge is a same-day problem. A stop-out at 9:40 becomes an oversized revenge trade at 9:50. Only a rule evaluation that runs on every entry catches this while it is happening.

What a day trading journal records

  • Trade log — symbol, direction, entry, exit, stop, size, P&L and R for every trade of the day.
  • Session — pre-market, open, RTH, midday, close. The single most useful dimension for day traders.
  • Setup — the pattern or condition that triggered each entry, so setups can be measured instead of debated.
  • Risk — dollar risk per trade and cumulative daily risk, checked against your daily budget before each entry.
  • Behavior — emotional state and rule adherence per trade, scored and recorded with violations.
  • Review — each trade is reviewable, and unreviewed trades are surfaced until you close the loop.

Performance analysis for day traders

With a full day's log, BattlexJournal computes the numbers that matter at day-trader volume:

  • Win rate, profit factor and expectancy — the honest summary of the day.
  • Performance by session and by setup, so you can cut what loses and scale what works.
  • Average R and equity curve — see trading performance analytics for the full list.
  • Risk usage per day against your limits — see trading risk management.
  • Discipline score tracking rule adherence — see trading discipline.

The nightly review

The journal is only half the system. The other half is reviewing the day while it is still fresh: which trades followed your plan, which didn't, and what to change tomorrow. BattlexJournal makes that review concrete — reviewed trades are marked, daily and weekly reviews are derived from your history, and your discipline score shows whether the fixes are sticking.
