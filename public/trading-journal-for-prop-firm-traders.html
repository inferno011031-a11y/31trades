Prop Firm Trading Journal

Trading Journal for Prop Firm Traders

A prop firm evaluation is a risk-management test as much as a trading test. BattlexJournal helps you monitor the things evaluations actually measure — daily risk, drawdown, discipline, trade frequency and your review process — without promising any particular result.

What an evaluation actually tests

Prop firm evaluations are usually lost on risk and behavior, not on analysis: a drawdown breached by a few bad days, a daily loss limit hit on a revenge trade, overtrading to recover. The journal's job is to make those risks visible before they end the evaluation.

Tracking the numbers that matter

BattlexJournal does not connect to your prop firm or track your evaluation account automatically. Instead, it tracks the behaviors from your journal that determine how evaluations go:

  • Daily risk. Set a daily risk budget in your account policy. Every trade consumes from it, and the pre-trade check warns you when a new trade would push past the limits you set. See trading risk management.
  • Drawdown. Current and maximum drawdown are derived from your equity curve and compared against your drawdown limit — the metric that ends most failed evaluations.
  • Trade frequency. The number of trades per day and per week is tracked. Overtrading is one of the most common evaluation killers, and it is invisible unless you count it.
  • Daily behavior. Your discipline score measures rule adherence across risk, strategy, execution, frequency, session and behavior — with each violation attached to the trade that caused it. See trading discipline.
  • The review process. Unreviewed trades are surfaced until you review them, and daily, weekly and monthly reviews are derived from your history. The review is where a bad week stops repeating. See how to review your trades.

Why prop firm traders keep a stricter journal

In a personal account, a rule break costs you money and you move on. In an evaluation, the same rule break can cost the entire account. The difference is not talent — it is how early you detect the behavior:

  • Revenge entries after a loss are visible as violations with timestamps.
  • Sizing up after a win is visible as risk escalation across consecutive trades.
  • Slow days where the evaluation stalls are visible in the calendar view of your trading activity.
  • Patterns — the same mistake on the same day of the week, the same setup, the same session — become measurable findings instead of vague suspicions.

Practice before you risk the evaluation

BattlexJournal includes trading replay and backtesting practice on historical market data, so you can test a new rule or a new setup before it costs evaluation capital. Practice trades are kept separate from your live journal.

A note on guarantees. BattlexJournal does not guarantee passing a prop firm evaluation, and no journaling tool can — results depend on your decisions and the market. What the journal does is make your risk, discipline and review process measurable, which is the part you control.
