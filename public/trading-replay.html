Trading Replay

Trading Replay & Market Replay

Replay is how you practice decisions without risking capital. BattlexJournal's Market Replay steps through historical price data bar by bar — you decide where to enter and exit, then review the session against what actually happened.

What replay means in BattlexJournal

Market Replay replays historical market data like a video: the chart starts hidden and reveals bars one at a time, exactly as they appeared in the market. You read the developing structure, decide when you would have entered and where you would have placed your stop, and continue the playback. At the end you see how your decisions would have performed.

  • Playback controls — play, pause, step, seek, reset and speed control, so you can stop at every decision point.
  • Real market history — sessions run on real historical candles, with a deterministic synthetic fallback when market data is unreachable.
  • No risk — replay positions are practice. They never touch your live account, your P&L or your risk state.

Why practice on replay

Most traders learn their setups on live charts, where the outcome is already visible and every decision feels obvious in hindsight. Replay removes the hindsight:

  • You see the market as it unfolded — one bar at a time, with no future bars to cheat from.
  • Decisions are timed. Entering at the right place on a finished chart is trivial; entering when the pattern is still forming is the actual skill.
  • Mistakes are cheap. A rule you keep breaking — early entries, missed stops — can be observed and corrected in practice before it costs real capital.
  • Volume is the teacher. A week of replay sessions produces more decision repetitions than a month of live trading.

Practice, separate from your live journal

Replay and backtesting practice records are kept strictly separate from your live ledger. Practice sessions have their own analytics and insights views, so practicing a new idea can never pollute your live performance numbers or your risk state. When a practice idea earns your confidence, you take it into the live journal with a clean record.

Review your replay sessions

A replay session without a review is just entertainment. Finished practice sessions produce results — equity curve, R distribution and breakdowns — and the AI coach reviews your decisions against your rules, pointing out where the practice behavior differed from the plan. That review loop is the same one your live journal uses: how to review your trades explains the framework.

Replay in your routine

Replay fits the improvement loop that runs through the whole platform:

  • Learn the setup structure on your journal history and analytics.
  • Practice the execution on replay, with rules from your risk and discipline system.
  • Review the sessions, adjust the plan, and repeat.
