How it works

Press Play to step through real historical candles bar by bar. The engine streams from the server, which pulls live TradingView data (cached for speed). With your TradingView account cookies set on the server (TRADEMIND_TV_SESSION + TRADEMIND_TV_SIGNATURE), playback uses TradingView's native replay mode — the same engine as the platform itself. Without them it replays the identical real history locally, one bar per tick. Use Reset to rewind, Step to move one bar, and the speed pills to control the pace.
