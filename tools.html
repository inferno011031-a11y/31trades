Projected equity path
—

Projection compounds the geometric expectancy: each trade grows the balance by ((1 + risk × avgWin) ^ winRate) × ((1 − risk × avgLoss) ^ lossRate). It is an average path, not a promise — variance is real and streaks are unavoidable.

Every number on this page is calculated locally in your browser from the inputs above. Contract and pip specs are CFD-standard — override the pip value on any instrument to match your broker.
